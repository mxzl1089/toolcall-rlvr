rewards_train.py is a local adaptation with typed equality, strict unique-block
parsing, multiset matching, and optimal unordered parameter assignment. It is
not the unmodified upstream reward and does not reproduce the original paper.
The original reward is only loaded read-only for audit tests.
The datasets and model remain at their existing local paths; publication must
retain their respective original licenses and attribution. Do not redistribute
the full local environment or model weights as part of the project repository.
